How can we help?

Connava support can help with account access, profile settings, privacy requests, account deletion, Community reports, blocked-user questions, Marketplace listings or transactions, safety concerns, and technical issues.

Email Connava Support

Support email: connavatravel@gmail.com

Include these details when relevant

  • the email address associated with your Connava account;
  • a short description of the issue;
  • the destination, trip, listing, review, or conversation involved;
  • screenshots that do not expose passwords, verification codes, or full payment details;
  • for Marketplace issues, the listing title and approximate transaction date.

Safety and reporting

Use in-app reporting tools when they are available for the content or listing at issue. For urgent physical danger or an emergency, contact local emergency services; Connava support is not an emergency-response service.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, email connavatravel@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request.”
